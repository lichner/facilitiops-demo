Questions

Get in touch
How does FacilitiOps work with FMX?

It reads your FMX data. FMX stays your system of record.

Can it run buildings that are not on FMX?

Yes, on the same screens and rules.

Who stays in control?

A named person approves anything sent to a client or contractor, or changed in FMX.

Where is my data, and how do people sign in?

Hosted on Microsoft Azure in Australia, with backups and processing in Australia. People sign in with their Microsoft work accounts. Your data is logically separated from every other customer.
